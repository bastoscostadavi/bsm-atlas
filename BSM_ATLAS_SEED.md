# BSM Atlas: initial 50-paper seed

Updated October 1, 2026.

The initial corpus contains **50 distinct papers, P01–P50**. The original six retain their IDs and are included in this total. The other 44 are part of the initial seed, not an optional backlog.

The selection develops an encyclopedia of diverse theory structures, including complicated constructions. It spans extended scalar and matter sectors, enlarged gauge groups and unification, supersymmetry, Peccei–Quinn and flavor symmetries, radiative structures, strong dynamics, extra dimensions, hidden sectors, and effective descriptions. Papers are selected for structural coverage and useful comparisons, not for solving a predetermined phenomenological problem.

Use the companion [agent prompt](BSM_ATLAS_AGENT_PROMPT.md) with this file. This seed file is the authoritative source manifest and extraction scope; the prompt supplies the workflow and evidence rules.

## Reading and extraction order

| Papers | Role in the seed |
|---|---|
| P01–P06 | Initial comparison set: reconciliation, interaction variants, supersymmetry and different descriptions. |
| P07–P20 | Scalar and matter representations, extended gauge sectors, anomaly classifications and unification. |
| P21–P34 | Supersymmetry, axion/PQ and flavor symmetries, singlet-fermion and radiative structures. |
| P35–P48 | Strong dynamics, naturalness constructions, extra dimensions, hidden sectors and model-representation tools. |
| P49–P50 | Effective-operator organization and relations between high- and low-energy descriptions. |

Process P01–P06 as the first iteration, then continue through the remaining sources in manageable batches until all 50 have been addressed. Revisit earlier records after justified schema changes. A paper can yield multiple specifications, and multiple papers can support one specification; the row count is not fixed at 50.

Reviews and classifications have bounded first-pass scopes below. Record which examples and sections were processed and which remain deferred. Tool papers inform the representation and retain source records, but are not themselves BSM models. Framework or charge-classification papers may support partial specifications or relations instead of complete theories.

## Papers and initial extraction scopes

### P01 — The Minimal Model of Nonbaryonic Dark Matter: A Singlet Scalar

**C. P. Burgess, M. Pospelov and T. ter Veldhuis (2000). [hep-ph/0011335v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0011335v3).**

*Initial comparison set · model.*

Begin with the model definition, scalar potential, symmetries and parameter conventions. This supplies a small, readable first record against which to test the extraction format. Its phenomenological motivation does not become an inclusion criterion for the atlas.

### P02 — Update on scalar singlet dark matter

**James M. Cline, Kimmo Kainulainen, Pat Scott and Christoph Weniger (2013). [1306.4710v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4710v5).**

*Initial comparison set · model.*

Compare its structural definition with P01. Determine which statements agree, which use different conventions, and which are study-specific assumptions or omissions. Do not assume in advance that every statement in the two papers describes an identical specification. This tests many-papers-to-one-model reconciliation and the difference between an omitted term and an explicitly absent term.

### P03 — Theory and phenomenology of two-Higgs-doublet models

**G. C. Branco et al. (2011). [1106.0034v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0034v3).**

*Initial comparison set · review.*

For the first pass, focus on **Type-I and Type-II Yukawa structures**, the scalar-sector assumptions relevant to those descriptions, and their symmetry realization. Record partial specifications if the discussion leaves choices open. Inventory other variants as unprocessed; do not attempt to catalogue the entire 180-page review now. This tests one-paper-to-many-model relations and the limits of identifying models by gauge group and representations alone.

### P04 — Left-Right Symmetry at LHC

**Alessio Maiezza, Miha Nemevsek, Fabrizio Nesti and Goran Senjanovic (2010). [1005.5160v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.5160v1).**

*Initial comparison set · model.*

Extract the gauge and matter structure, symmetry breaking, and the **parity and charge-conjugation implementations** of left-right symmetry. Record how these choices restrict interactions. The first pass concerns the structural definitions; numerical bounds remain attributed claims from the paper.

### P05 — The Next-to-Minimal Supersymmetric Standard Model

**Ulrich Ellwanger, Cyril Hugonie and Ana M. Teixeira (2009). [0910.1785v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.1785v5).**

*Initial comparison set · review.*

Start with the model-definition discussion of the **general and Z3-invariant NMSSM**, including field content, superpotential, soft terms and symmetry assumptions. Read surrounding material as needed to resolve definitions. Leave detailed phenomenology and the many additional scenarios for later. This tests superfields versus component fields, soft breaking, and distinctions among specifications within a named family.

### P06 — The Minimal Composite Higgs Model

**Kaustubh Agashe, Roberto Contino and Alex Pomarol (2004). [hep-ph/0412089v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0412089v2).**

*Initial comparison set · model.*

Extract the construction's symmetry structure, symmetry breaking, gauge embeddings and the relationship between its composite interpretation and five-dimensional realization. Record description regime, geometry or boundary information when needed. Preserve which statement belongs to which description. This tests whether the schema can accommodate theories beyond a list of elementary four-dimensional fields.

### P07 — The decoupling limit in the Georgi-Machacek model

**Katy Hartling, Kunal Kumar, Heather E. Logan (2014). [1404.2640v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2640v3).**

*Scalar sectors and additional matter · model.*

Extract the scalar representations, custodial symmetry action, general scalar potential and vacuum alignment of the Georgi–Machacek construction. Separate electroweak gauge representations from custodial multiplets after symmetry breaking. Record the decoupling assumptions as a parameter regime linked to the specification, rather than as a separate underlying theory.

### P08 — Classification of finite reparametrization symmetry groups in the three-Higgs-doublet model

**I. P. Ivanov, E. Vdovin (2012). [1210.6553v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.6553v2).**

*Scalar sectors and additional matter · classification.*

Record the classification domain and take two explicitly constructed symmetry cases as the first extraction targets. Capture how Higgs-family transformations and generalized CP act on the doublets and constrain the potential. Keep basis changes, realizability conditions and potential-level symmetry statements distinct from claims about a complete fermion sector.

### P09 — Flavor Changing Neutral Currents, an Extended Scalar Sector, and the Higgs Production Rate at the LHC

**Aneesh V. Manohar, Mark B. Wise (2006). [hep-ph/0606172v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0606172v3).**

*Scalar sectors and additional matter · model.*

Extract the color-octet electroweak-doublet scalar extension, including hypercharge convention, scalar interactions and quark couplings. Record the minimal-flavor-violation assumptions and any additional custodial restrictions separately. Keep extra matter mentioned for gauge unification as an optional extension unless the paper includes it in the specification being extracted.

### P10 — A handbook of vector-like quarks: mixing and single production

**J. A. Aguilar-Saavedra, R. Benbrik, S. Heinemeyer, M. Perez-Victoria (2013). [1306.0572v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0572v3).**

Preserve the assumption of predominant coupling to the third generation. Represent these choices as separate specifications or explicitly linked variants, and distinguish the gauge-eigenstate fields from masses and couplings after diagonalization.

### P11 — Physics of leptoquarks in precision experiments and at particle colliders

**I. Doršner, S. Fajfer, A. Greljo, J. F. Kamenik, N. Košnik (2016). [1603.04993v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04993v3).**

*Scalar sectors and additional matter · review.*

Begin with the representation and interaction taxonomy, extracting representative scalar and vector leptoquark descriptions with their baryon- and lepton-number assumptions. Mark simplified interaction descriptions and explicit ultraviolet completions separately. Do not infer a complete gauge theory from a massive vector representation alone; link each construction to its stated completion status.

### P12 — Minimal Dark Matter
